About Fleet Viewer

Fleet Viewer is an independent tool for exploring Star Citizen ships in your browser. Choose a manufacturer, load available ship models and compare their size side by side on a graduated hangar floor.

Compare ships at scale

Use the metric grid to judge ship dimensions, from small fighters to capital ships. Select a ship to move or rotate it, and place it back on the ground with one click. Models and dimensions may differ from the current game and can change during development.

Import your Star Citizen fleet

Import a HangarXPLOR JSON or CSV export, or a supported ship list. Recognized ships are selected in the catalogue; click Load to add them to the scene. Files are processed locally in your browser. Set quantities to compare multiple copies of a ship.

Explore the 3D hangar

Drag with the left mouse button to orbit, right-drag to pan and scroll to zoom. Click the scene to fly along your view with W/S, strafe with A/D and change altitude with Space/Ctrl. Press F to frame your selection or fleet, and Escape to release keyboard controls.

Fleet Viewer is a fan-made community project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Cloud Imperium Games or Roberts Space Industries. Star Citizen, ship designs and related trademarks belong to their respective owners. Using our referral link supports this project.
